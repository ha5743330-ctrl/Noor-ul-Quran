Honest limits

  • The free live site makes reels up to 35 seconds, 5 per day per account, one at a time.
  • Longer reels (up to 180 s) need the local app.
  • Generated files are deleted after 3 days. Download what you want to keep.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, within the daily limit. There are no ads.

Can I post the reels on social media?

Yes, keep the on-video credit and do not change the text. Selling them or commercial use is not allowed. See the Terms.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with email to create reels and keep a 3-day history.

Where does the Urdu text come from?

Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry (via AlQuran Cloud), or Mufti Taqi Usmani when the site owner has installed that text.
