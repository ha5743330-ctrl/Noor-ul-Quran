What we collect

  • Account: your email address (and the name you enter at signup), handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are never visible to us.
  • Profile: an optional display name and an optional profile photo. Photos are resized to 256×256 and stored privately in Supabase Storage; only you (and admins managing the service) can see them.
  • Your creations: the videos and images you generate, and a history list of them, are stored per user in Supabase Storage.
  • Usage counters: a daily count of videos per account (limit: 5 videos per day) is kept in server memory.

How long we keep it

Generated videos, images and their history entries are deleted automatically after 3 days. You can also delete any item yourself at any time from the Recent list. Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.

Who processes data

  • Supabase – authentication and file storage.
  • Render – runs the backend that renders videos.
  • Vercel – serves this website.

We show no ads and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Content sources

  • Recitation audio: everyayah.com.
  • Arabic text: Quran.com data, displayed in the KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs font.
  • English translation: Pickthall, via AlQuran Cloud.

Deactivating or deleting your account

From your Profile page you can:

  • Deactivate your account: you are signed out and cannot generate or view content until you sign in again and reactivate. Your files are kept.
  • Delete permanently (type DELETE and enter your password): we delete all your videos, ayah images, profile photo and history entries from storage, then your login account. This cannot be undone.
  • Download your data as a JSON file (profile and history list, no secrets).

Admins can block an account that breaks the Terms; the reason is shown to that user. Admin actions are recorded in an audit log (who, whom, action, time). You can also email contact@example.com from your registered address to ask for deletion.
